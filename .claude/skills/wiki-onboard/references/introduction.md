# Introduction script

Everything below the rule is the script. Output it word for word as your first
message of the session, then wait for the user's answer before starting step 1.

---

Welcome.

I'm a powerful tool, but I'm only as good as the context I can reach.

To make me a true sidekick and thinking partner — one that makes you many times more efficient — it's worth putting in the time to set up your workstation so working with me is seamless.

Think of me as a squirrel and context as acorns: I chase context relentlessly, following crumbs, doing whatever I can to fill out an idea and give you an answer. That's great when the acorns are well-structured, interlinked, relevant pieces of information. It's not so great when I have to guess my way to a conclusion because context is missing, or piece it together from scraps of bad context.

For most of us, that's exactly the situation: folders lying around your computer, data siloed in third-party tools — transcripts in Zoom, docs in Drive, emails in Outlook — and years of experience, best practice, and process locked up in our heads.

In April 2026, the famed AI researcher Andrej Karpathy wrote a viral [post pioneering an early version of an LLM knowledge base](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2039805659525644595) — a way to give structure to how we do knowledge work with AI.

Here's the approach: a folder of ordinary text files that link to each other. Point me at anything — a report, an email, a meeting transcript — and its raw material lands in a folder called **sources**, an inbox of sorts. On your word, or on a schedule, I distill that raw material into clean, well-structured pages in the **wiki** folder that always point back to the original. The result is a well-organised library of context that any future session with me can draw on and a scratchpad that your analysis can write to.

Unlike the knowledge bases of the past, the curation, data ingestion, and maintenance aren't driven by you — they're driven by me. You never edit anything in here by hand. Whatever you want done — adding material, changing a page, finding an answer — you ask me in your own words and I do the work.

Set the structure and guardrails today, hold my hand for a little while as I learn to operate in this space, and the payoff compounds: all your data, thoughts, and outputs in a single, well-organised place. A second brain, if you like. You'll find that most of your knowledge work going forward starts here — you will be able to sling reports, emails, posts, and plans with very little holding you back. 

Going forward, the AppGarden team will work with you in making this space the main place you operate in and get work done with Claude. Once it is well structured and filled with your data, it becomes fertile ground for automations, skill creation and artifact production.  

So if you're sold, today's job is getting the structure right — and it deserves your focus, so set aside up to an hour. I'll learn the words your team uses, propose a shape for the knowledge base, set up where your material will come from and let an army of agents bootstrap this space with the data needed to get going.

A few ground rules:

- Nothing today is permanent.
- We can stop any time and pick up where we left off.
- All data lives as files on your computer. Nothing in this directory is publicly available.
- Ask me if you get stuck - if you want to speak to a human don't hesitate to reach out to the AppGarden team at hello@appgarden.io

Ready to start?
