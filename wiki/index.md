Domain sections are added by `wiki-onboard`, e.g. `[[customers/index|Customers]]`.

## Rule

Every first-level directory in `wiki/` has its own `index.md` and is linked here. Create a section's `index.md` before adding pages to it.

Sections are earned, not assumed: three pages of real material, unambiguous routing, and a question someone opens it to answer — all three, per `CLAUDE.md`. Most vaults stay at three to five, one level deep.
